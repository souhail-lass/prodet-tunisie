# Content style guide — placeholder

> Status: Draft. Owner: Souhail. Last updated: 2026-05.

## Voice

- **Industrial.** Function and reliability over warmth.
- **Specific.** Real numbers, real sectors, real product use cases. No vague "world-class" / "best-in-class" claims.
- **Local.** Tunisian context (Aouina, Tunis, Grand Tunis delivery, Tunisian sectors).
- **Manufacturer-confident.** Lead with "we make this" where true; "we distribute" where not.
- **Calm, not loud.** No exclamation marks, no all-caps headlines.

## Forbidden words and patterns

- "Révolutionnaire", "innovant" without evidence.
- "Premier sur le marché" without proof.
- "AI-powered", "smart", "next-gen" anywhere on the public site (the AI lives inside).
- Emojis. Anywhere.
- Stock-photo "happy worker giving thumbs up."
- Unsubstantiated certifications.

## Copy patterns

- **Headlines.** 5–9 words. Sentence case (FR), Capital Initial (EN).
- **Sub-headlines.** One sentence, < 20 words.
- **CTA buttons.** Verb + object. "Demander un devis", "Voir le catalogue", "Nous contacter". Not "En savoir plus" alone.
- **Descriptions.** Short paragraphs (2–3 sentences). Use the Tunisian B2B vocabulary, not chemistry jargon.

## Product copy

- **Name (canonical)** displayed as-is from Swiver, in small/secondary type.
- **Localized name** (FR/AR/EN) is the primary display.
- **Short description** 1–2 sentences focused on use case ("Désinfectant chloré pour sols et sanitaires en milieu professionnel.").
- **Long description** technical detail, dilution recommendations, sector recommendations.

## Multilingual content rules

See [i18n-content-rules.md](i18n-content-rules.md).

## Voice in error and confirmation states

- Errors: explain what happened + what the user can do. Avoid blame ("Vous avez ..." → "Cette information est manquante : ...").
- Confirmations: brief and specific ("Demande de devis #PD-2026-00042 reçue. Nous vous répondons sous 24h ouvrées.").

## Voice in legal pages

- Plain language. Short sentences. Real names.
- No copy-pasted boilerplate that does not match our actual practice.
